01 — The house

Built in 1924. Kept quiet on purpose.

A captain’s house, a dining room added in the thirties, and a cedar loft in the old boathouse. We restored the plaster and the windows in 2019 and left the creak in the stairs.

There is no spa menu and no conference wing. The work of staying here is simple: sleep, eat, walk the headland, and come back before the light goes. If you need a television, the Library has a record player instead, which is the polite version of no.

The front room, which we still call the parlor. Coffee is set out here from half past six.

04 — A day

Nothing on the schedule is mandatory.

  1. 6:30Coffee in the parlor. The serious pot, not an urn.
  2. 8:30Breakfast until ten. Porridge, eggs, and whatever the bakery sent.
  3. 11:00Walk the headland. Forty minutes if you don’t stop, which you will.
  4. 1:00A long empty stretch. Read, nap, or drive into town for nothing in particular.
  5. 6:30Dinner at The Table. One sitting. Jackets are unnecessary; hunger is not.
